How scoring works. Each pain point is scored on three dimensions, Problem Identifier (5), Solution Designer (10) and Value Communicator (10), for 25 per pain point; four pain points make a case study score out of 100 and the four case studies a cumulative score out of 400. The auto-score reads the team's answers, matches the product they named against the answer key (including acceptable alternatives), and gauges the requirement and value against the model answer. It is a rubric-based estimate to start from, not a final grade: adjust any section before saving. The leaderboard is stored on this device only (use Export CSV to keep a record).
