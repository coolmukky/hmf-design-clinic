How scoring works. Each pain point is scored on three dimensions, Problem Identifier (5), Solution Designer (10) and Value Communicator (10), for 25 per pain point; four pain points make a case study score out of 100 and the four case studies a cumulative score out of 400. The auto-score reads the team's answers, matches the product they named against the answer key (including acceptable alternatives), and gauges the requirement and value against the model answer. It is a rubric-based estimate to start from, not a final grade: adjust any section before saving, or use Grade with AI for a rubric-aligned score from Claude. The leaderboard is shared across everyone who opens this page when the backend is connected (the pill by the leaderboard shows the state); otherwise it stays on this device.
